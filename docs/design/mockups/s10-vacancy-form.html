Notes

  • One form for creating and editing. A draft needs only a title; Publish checks the rest and names every missing field in red.
  • Skills are searched and added as rows; each has a Required toggle (Required or Preferred). Qualification, certification and other requirements are typed. At most 20 in total.
  • The closing date is a date picker limited to 89 days from today (or from the publish date when editing), so the server rule of 90 days always holds. The vacancy closes at the end of that day, Kampala time.
  • When the vacancy is published, title, type, place and how to apply are locked and shown disabled with an explanation.
  • Close always asks first and cannot be undone. A draft can be discarded the same way.
  • Publish is aria-disabled with the reason next to it until the company is approved; Save draft always works.
