Notes and rules

  • When things are saved: the server needs name, district, qualification, institution and study status together, so Basics is only checked, and Basics and Education are saved together when you press "Save and continue" on Education (the first save). After that every step saves on its own. Refreshing while still on Basics or Education loses those answers.
  • Skills, Experience and Summary can be skipped. The page opens at the first unfinished step (no skills: Skills; none added: Experience; otherwise Summary).
  • Steps you have not reached are locked (dashed, 🔒) until the first save; finished steps are always clickable.
  • "Still studying" shows Expected completion date; "Completed" shows Graduation date. Never both.
  • Institution: pick from the list, or choose "Other (type its name)" and type it. Field of study the same, and it is optional.
  • Limits shown as counters: 30 skills, 10 experiences, 1000 characters in the summary and in each description.
  • Errors from the server appear under the field; if the field is on another step the page jumps to that step and says so.
  • Leaving with unsaved changes (browser back, link, closing the tab) asks first.
  • Bottom navigation (new, phones, job seekers only): Jobs, Profile, Account. Applications is added with the applications screens. The page keeps space at the bottom so nothing is hidden behind it. Desktop uses the header links instead.
  • "CV and consent" is the sixth step, built later; it is not part of this step.
  • Inputs 16 px, labels always visible, dates use the phone's own date picker, tap targets 44 px.
