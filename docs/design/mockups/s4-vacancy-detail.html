Notes and states

  • Apply button by state: anonymous → "Log in to apply" (returns here after login); email not verified → disabled with "Verify your email to apply" and a resend link; profile or CV missing → "Complete your profile to apply" link; already applied → "Applied on 4 Nov" (no button); employer/admin → no Apply bar.
  • Phone: the Apply bar is sticky at the bottom; it never covers content (page gets bottom padding).
  • External vacancies: the notice is shown before the button; the link opens in a new tab with rel="noopener noreferrer" and is never counted as an application (C.3).
  • Required vs Preferred uses a text tag, not colour alone.
  • Dates in Africa/Kampala; salary integer UGX. Salary row hidden when the employer hides it.
  • Closed or expired vacancy: page still loads with a "This vacancy is closed" notice and no Apply.
  • Report link opens a Modal with a reason select and short text (Phase 4).
  • Loading: title and fact skeletons. Not found: "This vacancy is no longer available" with a link back to jobs. Error: ErrorState with Retry.
